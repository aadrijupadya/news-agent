RefineAnything: Multimodal Region-Specific Refinement for Perfect Local Details
The paper proposes a method to selectively refine specific regions of generated images using multimodal guidance (text, sketches, or reference images) without regenerating entire outputs. This is novel because it enables fine-grained control over image generation results, allowing users to fix imperfections or adjust details in localized areas while preserving the quality of surrounding content—addressing a key limitation of current generative models.
